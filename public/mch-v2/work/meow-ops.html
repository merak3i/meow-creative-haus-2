Frame Brief

Brief

Developers running Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Aider, Hermes and Antigravity spend real money on tokens with almost no view of what they got for it. Read the local session files, join them into one view, and keep every decision with the person.

Contribution

Product, interface and the open-source build: a self-hosted, installable PWA with five surfaces (Today, Review, Ledger, Sanctum, Learn), a focus timer on every screen, and no account or telemetry.

Deliverables & status

Open source on GitHub, MIT, v1.2.0. Reads local sessions from Claude Code, OpenAI Codex Desktop, Cursor, Aider, Hermes and Google Antigravity. Installs in two minutes with Node, no accounts.
