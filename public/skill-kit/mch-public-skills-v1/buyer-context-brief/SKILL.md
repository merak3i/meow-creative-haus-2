# Buyer context

## Inputs

The target industry or organization, buyer role, real offer and permitted sources.

## Workflow

1. Verify relevant public business signals. Separate observed facts from hypotheses about needs or timing.
2. Connect the offer to the role's likely responsibility using evidence; avoid unsupported claims about internal problems.
3. Draft one message with a specific reason for contact and a proportionate next step.
4. Mark missing facts that would change qualification. Do not infer sensitive personal traits or invent familiarity.
5. Prepare the handoff. Sending, enrichment purchases and CRM writes require the user's authorization for those actions.

## Deliver

A short qualification brief, supporting sources and an unsent outreach draft unless sending was authorized.

## Limits

No bundled prospect database or contact enrichment. Relevance is not a measured reply-rate improvement. Follow the user's current request and the host application's permissions. This instruction file grants no tool access or extra authority.

## Example request

Research this organization using public business sources. Prepare a buyer brief and one outreach draft. Label assumptions and do not send anything.
